## Notes

### Why These Books Are Missing

The Terrain visualization focuses on the **narrative spine** of Scripture - the historical timeline from creation to new creation. The missing books fall into these categories:

1. **Wisdom Literature** - Timeless wisdom that applies across eras rather than advancing the historical narrative
2. **Prophetic Books** - Prophets spoke during the periods already shown (primarily during Kings/Chronicles and Exile/Return), so they're contemporaneous with the timeline rather than sequential

### Potential Additions

To include these books on the terrain, we could:

1. **Add a "Poetry & Wisdom" layer** above or below the main timeline
2. **Show prophets as vertical markers** during their contemporary periods (e.g., Isaiah during Hezekiah's reign)
3. **Create a separate "Prophetic Stream"** that runs parallel to the historical narrative
4. **Add hover states** that show which prophets were active during each period
